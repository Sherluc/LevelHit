1. Introduction

Level Hit (hereinafter "we", "our company" or "the Studio") develops and publishes mobile games. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, share and protect your personal information when you use our mobile applications (hereinafter "the Games").

Data controller: Level Hit — contact: contact@levelhit.com

This policy is accessible at any time from the settings of each Game and from our website. It applies to all games published by Level Hit on the App Store (Apple) and Google Play Store.

2. Data Collected

Depending on the features used and permissions granted, we may collect the following categories of data.

  • Advertising identifiers : advertising identifier of the device (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android). Optional, subject to your consent.
  • Usage data : levels played, session duration, in-game progression.
  • Technical data : device model, operating system version, game version, configured language.
  • Diagnostic data : crash reports, error logs, performance indicators. Collected via Unity Diagnostics.
  • Network data : IP address (anonymized), network connection type.
  • Advertising data : ads displayed, impressions, clicks, conversions. Collected by our advertising partners, subject to your consent for the personalized portion.

3. Use of Data

We process your data for the following purposes, on the legal bases set out by the GDPR (Article 6) :

  • Game operation (progression, local save, bug fixes) — legal basis: performance of a contract (Art. 6.1.b).
  • Personalized advertising (targeted ads served via LevelPlay and our mediated networks) — legal basis: consent (Art. 6.1.a). You may withdraw consent at any time in the Game settings.
  • Non-personalized and contextual advertising (when no consent is given) — legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f), namely funding the free Game.
  • Diagnostics, crash reports, performance metrics, anti-fraud — legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f), namely Game stability and security.
  • Responding to GDPR/CCPA requests and legal compliance (responses to competent authorities) — legal basis: legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c).

4. Advertising & Mediation

Our Games are funded by mobile advertising. We use the LevelPlay (ironSource) platform as our advertising mediation solution, which coordinates multiple networks to optimize revenue and serve relevant ads.

If you reside in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom or California, we request your explicit consent via a banner before enabling personalized advertising. Consent is collected via a certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) compliant with IAB standards. You can withdraw this consent at any time in the Game settings.

Types of ads used :

  • Rewarded videos : optional videos you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward.
  • Interstitial ads : full-screen ads displayed between levels or at natural transitions in the game.
  • Banner ads : static or animated ads displayed at the edge of the screen during gameplay.

Data collected by the advertising system may include the device advertising identifier, device type, interactions with ads and approximate location (country / region).

6. Data Retention

We retain data only for the time necessary for the purposes described in section 3. Retention periods break down as follows :

  • Local preferences and progression (language, in-game progression, consent choices) : stored locally on your device until the Game is uninstalled or settings are manually reset.
  • Cloud-saved progression data (where this feature is available) : stored with our hosting provider for the duration of your use of the Game, and deleted after an extended period of inactivity (typically 24 months) or upon your request. The hosting provider and its safeguards will be specified here once the feature is activated.
  • Diagnostic and performance data (Unity Diagnostics) : retained by Unity under its own policy, typically up to 12 months.
  • Advertising data (identifiers, impressions, interactions) : retained by our advertising partners (ironSource/LevelPlay, Google AdMob, Unity Ads) under their respective policies referenced in section 5.
  • GDPR/CCPA request register and related correspondence : retained by Level Hit for 3 years from receipt, as proof of compliance.

To delete data collected by third-party services, uninstall the Game, reset your advertising identifier at the operating-system level, or use the deletion mechanisms offered directly by these services (see links in section 5).

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to, restrict, and port your personal data, as well as to withdraw your consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

California residents (CCPA/CPRA) : we do not sell your personal information. However, if you consent to personalized advertising, certain identifiers may be shared with our advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising, which qualifies as "sharing" under the CPRA. You can opt out at any time by disabling personalized ads in the in-game settings, or by writing to contact@levelhit.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information".

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address in section 11. We will respond within one month, extendable where permitted by law.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your data and collect only what is strictly necessary.

9. Protection of Minors

Our Games are intended for persons aged 13 and over and are declared as such on distribution stores. By using our Games, you declare that you are 13 or older. If you are between 13 and 16, you declare having obtained parental or guardian permission.

If we learn that a user is under 13, any collected data will be deleted as soon as possible. Parents or guardians may contact us at contact@levelhit.com.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. In the event of a material change, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of our Games after modification constitutes acceptance of the changes.
